# Demo Barbershop — knowledge base

This file is the source of truth for facts the agent may state. It is split into
chunks by `##` headings and retrieved with `search_knowledge`. The business owner
can edit it without touching code.

## Services and prices

All prices are in Kazakhstani tenge (KZT).

- Men's haircut (`haircut`): 6,000 KZT, 60 minutes.
- Beard trim and shaping (`beard`): 3,500 KZT, 30 minutes, booked as a 60-minute slot.
- Haircut + beard combo (`combo`): 8,500 KZT, about 80 minutes, done only by the senior master Arman.
- Kids haircut, under 12 years old (`kids`): 4,500 KZT, 60 minutes.
- Head shave with a straight razor (`shave`): 5,000 KZT, 60 minutes.

We do not offer hair coloring, perms, manicure or any women's services.

## Discounts

- Students get 10% off a men's haircut on weekdays before 14:00 with a valid student ID.
- There are no other discounts, promo codes or loyalty points. Staff cannot invent new discounts.

## Opening hours and address

- Open every day from 10:00 to 21:00. The last appointment starts at 20:00.
- Address: 12 Kabanbay Batyr Ave, Astana. Entrance from the courtyard, 1st floor.
- Free parking in the courtyard.

## Masters

- Arman: senior master, does all services including the combo.
- Dias: master, does haircut, beard, kids and shave. Dias does not do the combo.

## Booking rules

- To book we need the client's name and a Kazakhstan mobile number in the format +7XXXXXXXXXX.
- Bookings are possible from tomorrow up to 14 days ahead. Same-day bookings are by phone only.
- Each appointment takes one 60-minute slot starting on the hour.

## Cancellation and lateness

- Free cancellation or rescheduling up to 3 hours before the appointment.
- Later cancellations or no-shows: the next booking requires a 50% prepayment.
- If a client is more than 15 minutes late, the master may ask them to rebook.

## Payment

- We accept cash, bank cards and Kaspi QR.
- Payment is made after the service. Prepayment is only required after a late cancellation or no-show.

## Complaints, refunds and health issues

- Complaints, refund requests and any health-related issues (skin irritation, cuts, allergic reactions) are handled by the manager, not by the assistant.
- The assistant must hand such conversations to a human and must not promise compensation.
